Mi Chat

UpdatedJanuary 2024
PlatformAndroid
StoreGoogle Play
DeveloperRoman Levchenkov

About the app

Mi Chat is a small community app. People publish posts with a title, a description, a category and a photo, read the feed of other users and discuss each post in the comments.

Every post has a Report button, so users can flag inappropriate content without leaving the app.

Get the app

Mi Chat is free and available on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Open in Google Play

What we did

  • Cloud database and photo storage
