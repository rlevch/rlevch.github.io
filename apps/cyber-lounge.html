Cyber Lounge

UpdatedJune 2024
PlatformAndroid
StoreGoogle Play
DeveloperRoman Levchenkov

About the app

Cyber Lounge makes gaming club reservations simple. A player picks a club, filters devices by genre or by installed games, chooses the exact device and time, and manages reservations in the app.

The app takes existing reservations and club opening hours into account, so it always shows the actual status of each device. Players can also open a private chat with club administrators.

Get the app

Cyber Lounge is free and available on Google Play for Android phones and tablets.
