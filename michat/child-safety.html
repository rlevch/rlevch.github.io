Child Safety
Standards

App: Mi Chat (com.intellitechsolutions.michat) on Google Play. Developer: Roman Levchenkov. Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Mi Chat strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form. This includes any content or behaviour that sexualises minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any attempt to contact a minor for sexual purposes.

How users can report

Every post in Mi Chat has a Report button (flag icon) at the top of the post screen. A report can be sent without leaving the app. Users can also report any concern by email to intellitechstudio@gmail.com.

What we do with reports

  • Every report is reviewed by the developer as quickly as possible.
  • Content that violates these standards is removed and the account that posted it is permanently blocked.
  • Apparent child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant law enforcement and national authorities, as required by applicable law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

Compliance with law

Mi Chat complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the countries where the app is available.

Child safety contact

The designated point of contact for child safety questions about Mi Chat: Roman Levchenkov, intellitechstudio@gmail.com.
